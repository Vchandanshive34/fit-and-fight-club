Train with us

A weekly schedule for every individual, at every stage of life. Nine disciplines plus one-to-one coaching — taught separately, then put together.

Mixed Martial Arts

The full sport: striking, grappling and ground fighting drawn together from boxing, Muay Thai, wrestling and jiu jitsu. You start with the basics of each range and learn to move between them under pressure.

All levels · 14+

Muay Thai

The art of eight limbs. Fists, elbows, knees and shins, plus the clinch work that separates Thai boxing from every other stand-up style. Pad rounds every session.

All levels · 14+

Boxing

Footwork, guard, timing and combinations. The cleanest place to build fight fitness and the discipline everything else at the club is layered on top of.

All levels · 12+

Kick Boxing

The sharpness of martial arts with the agility of boxing. Fast, high-volume rounds on the bag and the pads — the most popular class for people training purely for fitness.

All levels · 12+

Wrestling

Clinch fighting, throws and take-downs, plus the scrambles that follow. Hard conditioning built in — the class most fighters credit for their engine.

Intermediate · 14+

Brazilian Jiu Jitsu

Taking an opponent to the ground, controlling them there, and finishing with a submission. Technical, low-injury, and the one discipline where size matters least.

All levels · 12+

Warrior Conditioning

Strength and conditioning built on functional movement — squat, hinge, push, pull, carry — run at an intensity that holds up under a fighter's workload. No martial arts experience needed.

All levels · 14+

HIIT

Short, hard anaerobic intervals against short recoveries. Forty-five minutes, in and out, for anyone whose main goal is fat loss and a working heart rate.

All levels · 14+

Karate for Kids

Strength, discipline and self-confidence, taught as a martial art rather than a workout. Structured belt progression so children can see how far they have come.

Children · 5–13

Personal training

One coach, one plan, one goal

Group classes take you a long way. A fight camp, a weight target with a date on it, or coming back from an injury needs something written for you alone. Personal training is available at all three centres, in packs of 8, 12, 16 or 24 sessions.

Getting started

  • Step 1Book a free trial class at the centre nearest you.
  • Step 2Turn up in shorts and a t-shirt. Gloves and pads are at the gym.
  • Step 3Train a full session with the group — not a watered-down taster.
  • Step 4Talk to the coach afterwards about which classes suit your goal.
