About the club

A mixed martial arts and fitness training centre that caters to every fitness need — weight loss or gain, self defence, or professional martial arts.

Our story

From 500 square feet to three centres

Fit and Fight Club was established in 2011 in Kharghar, Navi Mumbai, in a room of about five hundred square feet. In 2013 we opened a second floor in Seawoods. Since then the club has grown into three professionally managed centres — Vashi and Nerul in Navi Mumbai, and Wagholi in Pune.

Along the way we have trained more than 5,000 people: office workers who had never thrown a punch, children learning their first kata, and fighters preparing for a card. Every one of them gets a programme built for them, including modified work for members training around a health condition or an injury.

Our coaching team are practising MMA fighters from a mix of backgrounds — Wushu, Karate, Wrestling and Boxing. They train alongside the members, and they compete. The stated goal has never been modest: to put fighters from this club on the biggest platforms in the sport — ONE, Bellator, UFC.

Our philosophy

How we coach

Technique before intensity

Anyone can make a beginner tired. We would rather make you competent first — the conditioning arrives on its own once the movement is right.

Everyone spars eventually

Not on day one, and never carelessly. But a martial art you have only ever practised on a bag is a hobby, not a skill. We build up to contact carefully and supervise every round.

The programme fits the person

A fifty-year-old with a bad knee and a twenty-two-year-old in fight camp do not train the same way. Both get a plan. Both get watched.
